---
title: "3.8.0"
---

July 28, 2026

---

#### **Search**

We expanded what's searchable, and discoverable, across all of your video:

- **Find Anything, Anytime** - Until now, semantic search results have been limited to videos that already had a live detector running - you could search for anything, but you'd only find it if it shared the frame with a tagged object. We've expanded the search space to include all live video. Now you can find anything independently of the detections you already planned for, eliminating potential blind spots in investigation and streamlining discovery of the next object to tag.

---

#### **Overwatch**

We're continuing to extend the sightlines of our live monitoring view, making it sharper across the board:

- **Object Tracking in Timeline** - Objects now render directly in the timeline with improved tracking performance, so you can see activity at a glance.
- **Advanced Event Filtering** - New filtering options make it easier to monitor for or find exactly the events that matter.
- **Event Details & Validation** - View and validate event details all from a single screen.

---

#### **Model Training**

We're improving both the precision of what we capture as well as the speed of the whole pipeline:

- **Prompt-Based Segmentation** - Use keywords to segment only the objects you want instead of everything in frame, so tagging is faster with less noise.
- **Faster Pipeline** - Speed boosts to training and model metrics jobs get you to a deployed model faster - enabling more iteration and more accurate models, sooner than before.

---

#### **Platform Performance**

We also shored up reliability and security across the platform. Devices with bad storage credentials now fail clearly instead of retrying silently, a single broken dashboard widget no longer takes down the rest of the view, video processing and stability issues were resolved, and we patched several vulnerabilities in data handling, cookie management, and third-party dependencies to keep your deployments secure.
