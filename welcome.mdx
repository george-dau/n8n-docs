Worlds turns your physical operations, the equipment, people, and processes that make up your day-to-day, into real-time, actionable intelligence. Using cameras and sensors you likely already have in place, Worlds gives your organization visibility into what's actually happening on the ground, and the tools to act on it immediately.

## Why It Matters

Most organizations can measure what comes in and what goes out, but everything that happens in between, on the floor, at the dock, on the line, is largely invisible. Without that visibility, improvement is guesswork. Worlds closes that gap: customers have seen reductions of 30-50% in manual quality inspections, and a 19% year-over-year drop in safety incident rates through continuous AI monitoring.

## How It Works

Worlds operates in three stages:

- **See Everything** - pull real-time data from your existing cameras and sensors, at scale.
- **Understand More** - turn that data into spatial intelligence that surfaces patterns, bottlenecks, and opportunities.
- **Act Faster** - deploy automation, agents, and workflows that keep improving your operations continuously.
