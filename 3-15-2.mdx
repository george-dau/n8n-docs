November 2, 2026

---

#### **Dashboards & Iris**

Dashboard builds you can follow from start to finish, and video right in the conversation:

- **Dashboard Build Progress** - When Iris or an agent builds a dashboard, you can watch its progress as it works, and the build keeps going if you leave or reload the page. Finished builds land as a draft you can review and publish, and if a build fails, you see what went wrong and can try again.
- **Empty Widgets Stay Put** - Widgets with no data yet now show a clear empty state instead of being left off the dashboard, so the layout you asked for is the layout you get.
- **Video in Iris Chat** - Ask Iris for a live camera or the video behind an event, and it plays right in the chat. If your request could mean more than one camera or event, Iris offers choices instead of guessing.

---

#### **Tracking**

Tracking tuned to the environment:

- **Tracker Profiles** - Each camera can now run its own tracking profile. The first, **Drive-Thru**, cuts vehicle tracking errors by about 60% in testing, and the default tracker picked up accuracy improvements along the way. The Worlds team sets these up for you today, and more profiles with finer control are on the way to improve tracking across all kinds of cameras and conditions.

---

#### **Performance**

Less waiting, with fewer gaps in data:

- **Faster Overwatch and Event Activity** - Both pages load much faster on busy sites, and pages you leave stop loading in the background so the next one opens sooner.
- **Faster Camera Restarts** - Restarting a camera now takes seconds, so recording picks back up almost right away.
- **Broader Hardware Support** - Edge detectors and transcoders now come in NVIDIA, AMD, and CPU-only versions, so Worlds can be deployed on a wider range of hardware and CPU-only installs are a fraction of the size.
