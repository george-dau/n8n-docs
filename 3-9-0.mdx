August 10, 2026

---

#### **Event Activity**

We rebuilt the Events experience top to bottom - a new list view and embedded detail view working together, so you can find what you need without every breaking the experience by navigating away, reloading the page, or losing your place: 

- **Two-Panel Layout** - The Events page now uses a redesigned two-panel layout, so you can browse and inspect events side by side.

---

#### **Overwatch**

We continued iterating on our live map view into facility activity - making it more performant, and preparing for additional future enhancements: 

- **Improved Clustering** - Objects on the map now group more clearly at scale, so busy scenes stay readable.
- **Better Off-Map Track Handling** - Tracks that move off-screen or off-map are now handled more gracefully.
- **Enhanced Map Control** - Right-click and drag to pan and tilt the map view, for a clearer look at object and event positions.

---

#### **Platform Performance**

We're always squashing bugs behind the scenes that often go unnoticed (we hope), but two recent performance wins worth mentioning: We've updated our default training model so that your models start from a stronger baseline before your data even enters the picture. And we've begun implementing transcoder telemetry reporting to better support customers' edge deployments.
