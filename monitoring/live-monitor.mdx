Live Monitor, under Monitoring in the navigation, is a simple way to pull up and review live video from any number of cameras at once. It's a companion to [Overwatch](/monitoring/overwatch), not a replacement for it: Overwatch is Worlds' single pane of glass for live events and reacting to them, Live Monitor is just for watching live video.

## Before You Start

- Worlds isn't intended to be a full video management system (VMS), and typically runs alongside an existing VMS rather than replacing it. Live Monitor is a lightweight companion view, though it can double as a simple VMS-style reference if you need one.

## Step 1: Open Live Monitor

Clicking Live Monitor in the navigation defaults to opening the first four cameras at your currently selected site.

## Step 2: Choose a Layout

Two things to set at the top of the page:

- **Fit or Fill** - fit each camera's full frame into its box, or fill the box entirely with the frame
- **Template** - choose from a single camera, a 4-camera panel, one large panel with four smaller panels, or a full 9-panel grid

\[SCREENSHOT: Layout and template controls\]

## Step 3: Choose Your Cameras

Use the right panel to control which cameras appear in each frame: click the toggle to add a camera, or click an already-enabled camera to remove it.

Use the filter menu to narrow the list to all cameras, cameras currently in view, or only cameras available with an enabled live stream.

\[SCREENSHOT: Camera selection panel with filters\]

## Step 4: Work with an Active Frame

Hover over any frame with a live stream to reveal a few options:

- Click and drag to move a camera to a different panel
- Click the full screen icon (top right of the frame) to expand that panel
- Use the ellipses menu to manage which camera is assigned to that frame, or open the camera's details

\[SCREENSHOT: Hovering a live frame showing the options overlay\]

## Step 5: Switch to Map View

Click Map View for the same panel functionality, but with cameras plotted on a map instead of a list. Cameras need a location configured in [Builder](/sites-and-cameras/builder) to appear here.

\[SCREENSHOT: Live Monitor map view\]

## Step 6: Go Full Screen

Click the small arrow in the bottom right to collapse the camera selection panel and expand your video view to fill the screen.

## Common Use

[Overwatch](/monitoring/overwatch) is the better fit for live monitoring across a large number of cameras. But Live Monitor works well as a lightweight, pseudo-VMS view: build a panel of cameras and leave it running on a screen or TV for a constant, at-a-glance reference of what's happening across a facility, multiple cameras streaming simultaneously, in real time.

## Quick Reference

- **Default view** - first four cameras at your selected site
- **Fit/Fill** - frame sizing toggle
- **Templates** - 1, 4, 4\+1, or 9-panel layouts
- **Camera selection** - toggle on/off from the right panel, filterable by view or live-stream status
- **Active frame options** - drag to reposition, full screen, or manage/open details via the ellipses menu
- **Map view** - same functionality, camera locations plotted on a map (requires [Builder](/sites-and-cameras/builder)-configured location)
- **Best for** - lightweight reference and pseudo-VMS use; [Overwatch](/monitoring/overwatch) is the primary tool for large-scale live monitoring
